Privacy Policy — TruckerRoute
Last updated: February 2026

1. Overview

TruckerRoute ("we", "our", "the app") is a real-time road hazard and weather map for truck drivers. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our app and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

When you launch the app and accept the Terms of Service, we receive the following anonymous technical data:

Anonymous device identifier (Apple's Vendor ID — resets if the app is deleted and reinstalled)
App version
iOS version
Device language and timezone
This data does not identify you personally and cannot be linked back to you. It is used solely to understand how many devices are actively using the app and which app versions are in use.

3. Location Data

The app requests access to your device location to display your position on the map (the blue dot). Your location is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

Your name, email address or any personal identifiers
Your precise or historical location
Any information about your routes or driving behavior
Payment information
Contacts, photos or any other device content
5. Third-Party Services

Mapbox provides the map tiles rendered in the app. When the map loads, your device makes requests to Mapbox servers. Please refer to the Mapbox Privacy Policy (https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy) for information on how Mapbox handles those requests.

All road, weather and hazard data is sourced from public US government APIs (NOAA, NWS, NASA, USGS, NIFC, state DOT 511 networks) and does not involve any data collection from you.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous usage data (device identifier, app version, iOS version, language, timezone) is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.
