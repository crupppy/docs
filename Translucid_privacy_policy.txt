# Privacy Policy

**Voice Translator**  
*Last Updated: January 12, 2026*

---

## 1. Introduction

Voice Translator ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Audio Data
- We capture audio through your device's microphone for speech recognition
- Audio is processed in real-time and is **not stored** on any server

### 2.2 Translation Data
- Text generated from speech recognition is processed for translation
- Translations are stored **locally on your device only** in the history feature
- You can delete all history at any time from Settings

### 2.3 Usage Data
- We track the duration of translation usage for the subscription system
- This data is stored in your iCloud account and synced across your devices

## 3. How We Process Your Data

### On-Device Mode
When "On-Device Recognition" is enabled:
- All speech recognition happens locally on your device
- No audio data leaves your device

### Online Mode
When "On-Device Recognition" is disabled:
- Audio may be sent to remote servers for enhanced processing
- Provides higher accuracy for speech recognition

## 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do **NOT**:
- Store your audio recordings on any server
- Share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
- Sell your personal information
- Track your location
- Collect personal identifiable information

## 5. Data Storage

| Data Type | Storage Location | Retention |
|-----------|------------------|-----------|
| Audio | Not stored | Immediately discarded |
| Translation history | Local device | Until you delete it |
| Usage balance | iCloud | Permanent |
| Preferences | Local device | Until app is deleted |

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View your translation history in the App
- **Delete**: Clear all history from Settings
- **Control**: Choose between On-Device and Online recognition modes

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data:
- All network communications use encryption (HTTPS/TLS)
- Local data is protected by your device's security features

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store.
