# Privacy Policy

**Voice Translator**  
*Last Updated: January 13, 2026*

---

## 1. Introduction

Voice Translator ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Audio Data
- We capture audio through your device's microphone exclusively for the purpose of speech recognition and translation.
- Audio processing is performed using native operating system technologies.

### 2.2 Translation Data
- Text generated from speech recognition is processed for translation using built-in system translation technologies.
- Translations are stored **locally on your device only** in the history feature.
- You can delete all history at any time from the internal Settings menu.

### 2.3 Usage Data
- We may track session duration to implement service limitations (e.g., session time limits).
- This technical data may be stored in your private cloud storage associated with your device account to sync usage status across your devices. We do not have access to this data.

## 3. How We Process Your Data

### Speech Recognition & Translation
- The App utilizes native frameworks provided by the operating system for processing.
- When "On-Device Recognition" is valid and enabled, speech processing happens locally.
- In some cases, or for enhanced accuracy, data may be processed by the operating system provider's servers in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

## 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do **NOT**:
- Store your audio recordings on our servers.
- Share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
- Sell your personal information.
- Track your precise location.
- Collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as name, email, or phone number.

## 5. Data Storage

| Data Type | Storage Location | Retention |
|-----------|------------------|-----------|
| Audio | Not stored | Immediately discarded after processing |
| Translation history | Local device | Until you delete it |
| Usage/Session status | Cloud Storage (Private) | Needed for app functionality |
| Preferences | Local device | Until app is deleted |

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View your translation history in the App.
- **Delete**: Clear all history from the Settings menu.
- **Control**: toggle settings related to speech recognition modes.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. Since the App relies heavily on on-device processing, your data benefits from the strong security protections built into the operating system.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App or on our App Store page.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store support page.
