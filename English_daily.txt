Privacy Policy
Effective Date: December 18, 2025

English
10 Words: English Daily ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains that we do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

1. Data Collection
We do not collect any personal data.

Progress & Settings: All your learning progress, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device (via UserDefaults).
No Tracking: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking systems.
Voice Data: Voice input is processed in real-time on your device (using Apple's On-Device Speech Recognition where available) solely for the purpose of scoring pronunciation. We do not upload or store your voice recordings on any servers.
2. Permissions
The App may request access to:

Microphone & Speech Recognition: Necessary to listen to your pronunciation during tests. This data is not shared with third parties.
Notifications: Used solely to send you daily reminders to learn new words. You can disable this in your device settings at any time.
